DIOSCORIDES, De materia medica - French translation
SELECTED: Martin Mathée, Les six livres de Pedacion Dioscoride d'Anazarbe de la matière
médicinale, translatez de latin en françois, with his annotations; Lyon, Balthazar
Arnoullet, 1553 (first edition of this translation).
SLOT: FRENCH.

FILES (all in the source/ folder next to this note, exactly as the libraries delivered them)
- lessixlivresdepe00dios_jp2.zip (+ lessixlivresdepe00dios_metadata.json)
  What: University of Toronto (Fisher Library) copy, via Internet Archive, 438 images,
  about 2115 x 3155 px.
  Why here: the French text will be typed from this scan (rule 2: no reliable typed text).

WHY THIS VERSION
- 5 French versions in the edition map: 2 are extracts (1566, 1569), so incomplete. The
  3 others are Mathée's translation, printed 1553, 1559 and 1580: all free of copyright,
  none with a reliable typed text, all old printings with annotations, none bilingual.
- So the earliest edition with a sharp-enough downloadable scan wins (rule 5.6): 1553.
  It passes rule 4 (complete, free, downloadable in full) and is sharp enough.
- It replaces the 1580 printing downloaded earlier on 12 November 2026, chosen when the
  last rule was "sharpest scan" instead of "earliest edition"; that folder was deleted.

RIGHTS: text public domain; Internet Archive states no licence for the scan.

FILE TYPES
- *_jp2.zip : one volume's page images (JPEG 2000), exactly as Internet Archive provides them.
- *_jpg.zip : one volume's page images (JPEG), as the library serves them page by page,
  packed without recompression.
- *_metadata.json / *_manifest.json : the library's own record of the item, unchanged.
- *_pages.csv : one row per page image with its checksum (for *_jpg.zip).
- *.xml : a typed text made by a scholarly project (TEI format).
- *.pdf : a scan delivered as a PDF.
