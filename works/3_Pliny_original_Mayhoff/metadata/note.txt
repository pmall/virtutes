WHY THIS VERSION
- 101 Latin versions in the edition map: 8 incomplete, 4 under copyright. Mayhoff is the
  only one with a reliable typed text (rule 5.2; rule 3: Perseus corrects its texts).
  Other typed texts do not qualify: Corpus Corporum (Jan's 1854 text) states no licence;
  LacusCurtius is only partly proofread; Latin Wikisource gives no source.

NOT VERIFIED
- The page numbers of volumes I, II and V against their scans.

RIGHTS: text public domain (Mayhoff died 1914); typed text CC BY-SA 4.0; scans not in
copyright.

FILE TYPES
- *_jp2.zip : one volume's page images (JPEG 2000), exactly as Internet Archive provides them.
- *_jpg.zip : one volume's page images (JPEG), as the library serves them page by page,
  packed without recompression.
- *_metadata.json / *_manifest.json : the library's own record of the item, unchanged.
- *_pages.csv : one row per page image with its checksum (for *_jpg.zip).
- *.xml : a typed text made by a scholarly project (TEI format).
- *.pdf : a scan delivered as a PDF.
