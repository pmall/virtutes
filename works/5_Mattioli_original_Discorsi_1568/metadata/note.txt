WHY THIS VERSION
- Rule 5.1, benchmark edition: Staub, Casu and Leonti 2016 (Phytomedicine), "De Materia
  Medica (ex Matthioli, 1568)", 5,314 therapeutic uses extracted by line-by-line reading of
  the five-volume facsimile printed in Rome, 1967-1970 (checked in the article,
  references/Staub_2016_Phytomedicine.pdf). Its scan was judged sharp enough. The 21 other Italian versions
  were therefore not considered.

RIGHTS: not in copyright (Internet Archive).

FILE TYPES
- *_jp2.zip : one volume's page images (JPEG 2000), exactly as Internet Archive provides them.
- *_jpg.zip : one volume's page images (JPEG), as the library serves them page by page,
  packed without recompression.
- *_metadata.json / *_manifest.json : the library's own record of the item, unchanged.
- *_pages.csv : one row per page image with its checksum (for *_jpg.zip).
- *.xml : a typed text made by a scholarly project (TEI format).
- *.pdf : a scan delivered as a PDF.
