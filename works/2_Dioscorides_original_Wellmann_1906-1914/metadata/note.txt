SELECTED: Max Wellmann's critical edition, Berlin, Weidmann, 1906-1914, 3 volumes.
SLOT: ORIGINAL (Greek). ENGLISH: empty (see below). FRENCH: see folder
2_Dioscorides_french_Mathee_1553.

FILES (all in the source/ folder next to this note, exactly as the libraries delivered them)
- tlg0656.tlg001.1st1K-grc1.xml
  What: Wellmann's Greek text of the five books, typed by the First1KGreek project
  (CC BY-SA 4.0).
  Why here: it is the text we work from (rule 2).
- b21459162_0001_jp2.zip, b21459162_0002_jp2.zip, b21459162_0003_jp2.zip (+ *_metadata.json)
  What: scans of volumes 1-3 (278, 382 and 416 images), University of Glasgow Library copy,
  via Internet Archive.
  Why here: scan of the same edition as the typed text (rule 2). The typed text gives
  Wellmann's page numbers; checked on volume 2, page 100 (image 134): same first words.
  Each page is matched to its image by convert/images_grc.py, which states how it was checked;
  the text itself is made without these scans.

WHY THIS VERSION
- 9 versions in the edition map, all complete and free of copyright. Wellmann is the only
  one with a reliable typed text (rule 5.2; rule 3: First1KGreek corrects its texts).
  The others were not reached.
- English: empty (rule 6). 10 versions: 1 incomplete (selection), 5 under copyright
  (Osbaldeston 2000, Beck 2005 and its reprints). Goodyer's translation edited by Gunther
  (1934, reprints 1959, 1968, 1996) is free of copyright in the EU (Gunther died 1940) but no
  copy can be downloaded in full: Internet Archive lending only, HathiTrust search only,
  Wellcome not digitised, Google Books no full view, Biodiversity Heritage Library no title.

NOT VERIFIED
- Nothing left about these files (the page numbers are checked in convert/images_grc.py).

RIGHTS: text public domain (Wellmann died 1933); typed text CC BY-SA 4.0; scans CC BY-NC 4.0
(Wellcome Collection record).

FILE TYPES
- *_jp2.zip : one volume's page images (JPEG 2000), exactly as Internet Archive provides them.
- *_jpg.zip : one volume's page images (JPEG), as the library serves them page by page,
  packed without recompression.
- *_metadata.json / *_manifest.json : the library's own record of the item, unchanged.
- *_pages.csv : one row per page image with its checksum (for *_jpg.zip).
- *.xml : a typed text made by a scholarly project (TEI format).
- *.pdf : a scan delivered as a PDF.
