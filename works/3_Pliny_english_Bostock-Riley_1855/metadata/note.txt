  What: the whole translation, typed by the Perseus Digital Library (CC BY-SA 4.0).

- Scans of the six volumes (+ *_metadata.json), MBLWHOI Library copy, via Internet Archive,

WHY THIS VERSION
- 12 English versions in the edition map: 8 incomplete (abridgements, selections),
  1 under copyright (Loeb). Two complete free versions have a reliable typed text:
  Bostock & Riley (Perseus) and Holland's 1634 printing (EEBO-TCP, proofread). They tie at
  rule 5.2; Bostock & Riley is a scholarly edition and Holland's an old printing, so
  Bostock & Riley wins at rule 5.4.

NOT VERIFIED
- The page numbers of volumes 1, 2, 4, 5 and 6 against their scans.

RIGHTS: text public domain; typed text CC BY-SA 4.0; scans not in copyright.

FILE TYPES
- *_jp2.zip : one volume's page images (JPEG 2000), exactly as Internet Archive provides them.
- *_jpg.zip : one volume's page images (JPEG), as the library serves them page by page,
  packed without recompression.
- *_metadata.json / *_manifest.json : the library's own record of the item, unchanged.
- *_pages.csv : one row per page image with its checksum (for *_jpg.zip).
- *.xml : a typed text made by a scholarly project (TEI format).
- *.pdf : a scan delivered as a PDF.
