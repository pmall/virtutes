CIRCA INSTANS (Liber de simplici medicina, Salerno school, 12th century)
SELECTED: the Venice edition of 16 December 1497, printed by Bonetus Locatellus for
Octavianus Scotus, in a volume with Serapion's works (Practica, Liber de simplici medicina),
Galen and Johannes Platearius' Practica; folio, 211 leaves.
SLOT: ORIGINAL (Latin).

FILES (all in the source/ folder next to this note, exactly as the libraries delivered them)
- bsb00061068_jpg.zip
  What: the Circa instans leaves only, 186r to 211v (52 page images, about 3410 x 5077 px),
  from the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek copy, call number 2 Inc.c.a. 3538. Leaf 186r begins
  "Incipit liber de simplici medicina secundum Platearium dictus Circa instans"; leaf 211v
  ends "Explicit liber de simplicibus medicinis" followed by the printer's colophon.
- bsb00061068_manifest.json: the library's page list (leaf numbers for every image).
- bsb00061068_pages.csv: each image with its leaf number and checksum.
  Why here: the Latin text will be typed from these scans, each passage keeping its leaf
  number (rule 2).

WHY THIS VERSION
- 25 Latin versions in the edition map (including the Wellcome manuscript MS.624):
  4 incomplete, 3 under copyright.
- No reliable typed text (Gallica's Paris 1564 copy has only automatic OCR, 23%; Corpus
  Corporum has Platearius' Practica only).
- First on paper, Wölfel's scholarly edition (1939, rule 5.4) cannot be downloaded anywhere.
- Printed books come before the manuscript (rule 5.3). The Lyon printings with Michael de
  Capella's additions count as old printings (decided 12 November 2026).
- Among the old printings, Venice 1497 is the earliest with a sharp-enough downloadable scan
  (rule 5.6).
- It replaces the Paris 1582 printing downloaded earlier on 12 November 2026; that folder
  was deleted.

RIGHTS: Public Domain Mark (Bayerische Staatsbibliothek).

FILE TYPES
- *_jp2.zip : one volume's page images (JPEG 2000), exactly as Internet Archive provides them.
- *_jpg.zip : one volume's page images (JPEG), as the library serves them page by page,
  packed without recompression.
- *_metadata.json / *_manifest.json : the library's own record of the item, unchanged.
- *_pages.csv : one row per page image with its checksum (for *_jpg.zip).
- *.xml : a typed text made by a scholarly project (TEI format).
- *.pdf : a scan delivered as a PDF.
