THEOPHRASTUS, Enquiry into Plants (Historia plantarum)
SELECTED: Arthur Hort, Loeb Classical Library, London 1916, 2 volumes (Greek on the left-hand
pages, English on the right-hand pages).
SLOTS: ORIGINAL (Greek) and ENGLISH. FRENCH: empty.

FILES (all in the source/ folder next to this note, exactly as the libraries delivered them)
- tlg0093.tlg001.1st1K-grc1.xml
  What: Hort's Greek text, books I-IX, typed by the First1KGreek project (CC BY-SA 4.0).
  Why here: it is the Greek text we work from (rule 2: a reliable typed text is taken).
- Theophrastus_Enquiry_Plants_Loeb.pdf (+ theophrastus-enquiry-plants-loeb_metadata.json)
  What: scan of both volumes, 1,014 pages, about 2520 x 3786 px: the original PDF of the
  Internet Archive item theophrastus-enquiry-plants-loeb. The file came from the former
  pilot folder, not from a download by the project's tools; checked on 17 November 2026:
  its size and checksum are those Internet Archive publishes for that PDF, and it now has
  the library's file name.
  Why here: (1) the English exists only as this scan and will be typed from it;
  (2) it is the scan of the same edition as the typed Greek (rule 2). The typed Greek gives
  Hort's printed page numbers: printed page 6 = PDF page 31 in volume 1; volume 2 restarts.

WHY THIS VERSION
- Original: 28 versions in the edition map; 8 incomplete, 2 under copyright. Hort 1916 is the
  only one with a reliable typed text (rule 5.2: First1KGreek is a scholarly project that
  corrects its texts, rule 3). The others were not reached.
- English: the 8 English versions are all Hort's translation; 5 are single volumes of
  reprints (incomplete). Of the complete ones (1916, 1948-1949, 1961), 1916 is the earliest
  with a sharp-enough downloadable scan (rule 5.6). No reliable typed text of the English
  exists (Perseus and First1KGreek have none; the English Wikisource transcription is
  incomplete).
- French: empty (rule 6). 5 versions: 3 incomplete; the 2 complete ones (Amigues, Budé
  1988-2006, and Belin 2010) are under copyright.

RIGHTS: text public domain (Hort died 1935); typed Greek CC BY-SA 4.0.

FILE TYPES
- *_jp2.zip : one volume's page images (JPEG 2000), exactly as Internet Archive provides them.
- *_jpg.zip : one volume's page images (JPEG), as the library serves them page by page,
  packed without recompression.
- *_metadata.json / *_manifest.json : the library's own record of the item, unchanged.
- *_pages.csv : one row per page image with its checksum (for *_jpg.zip).
- *.xml : a typed text made by a scholarly project (TEI format).
- *.pdf : a scan delivered as a PDF.
